# PersonaMirror369 Claim Boundaries

PersonaMirror369 is a humane, local-first reflection and education project. This document defines what the project does and does not claim.

## What PersonaMirror369 Is

PersonaMirror369 is a software application and conceptual framework for:

- personal reflection;
- journaling;
- noticing social pressure;
- exploring everyday roles and masks;
- practicing consent-centered language;
- planning gentle integration steps;
- exporting and managing local browser data.

## What PersonaMirror369 Is Not

PersonaMirror369 is not:

- therapy;
- medical care;
- mental health diagnosis;
- a clinical screening tool;
- a lie detector;
- a personality test;
- a social scoring system;
- a workplace surveillance system;
- a coercive persuasion system;
- proof of a universal model of human behavior.

## Reflection Scores and Formulas

The project includes symbolic and practical formulas such as Pretending Force, Mask Load, Coherence Delta, Group Pretending Field, and Manipulation Pressure.

These formulas are design tools and reflection aids. They are not validated clinical measures, scientific instruments, or predictive models.

## Local-First Boundary

The app is designed around browser-local storage and user-controlled export. Users should still understand that browser storage is controlled by their device and browser environment.

The app does not promise absolute privacy, security, or permanence. Users should export important reflections if they want to preserve them.

## Consent Boundary

PersonaMirror369 should be used for self-reflection and consent-based conversation. It should not be used to force disclosure, pressure someone to agree, profile people without consent, or punish people for having masks.

## Public-Safe Summary

PersonaMirror369 helps people notice masks with mercy. It does not rip masks off people. It helps people feel safe enough to set them down.
