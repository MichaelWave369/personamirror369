# PersonaMirror369: A Consent-Based Framework for Mapping Social Masks and Reducing Pretending Force

**Version:** v0.1 genesis draft  
**Status:** Research scaffold / design framework / interactive software concept  
**Public subtitle:** A humane atlas for the masks we wear.

## Abstract

PersonaMirror369 proposes a consent-based framework for mapping the social masks people wear, estimating the pressures that create and sustain those masks, and designing humane tools that help individuals and groups return toward truthful coherence. The framework treats masks not as simple deception, but as adaptive interfaces between private experience and public performance. Some masks are useful, protective, and temporary. Others become rigid, chronic, fear-driven, and identity-fused.

The project introduces three early models: the Mask Atlas, the Pretending Force Engine, and the Mask Load Model. These models are intended for reflection, education, social literacy, anti-manipulation awareness, and ethical AI companion design. PersonaMirror369 explicitly rejects coercive profiling, forced disclosure, diagnosis, surveillance, social scoring, and manipulative persuasion.

## 1. Introduction

Every person wears masks. A person may act confident at work, agreeable with family, funny in public, spiritual in community, tough under pressure, or invisible in unsafe rooms. These masks may be conscious or unconscious. They may protect belonging, reduce danger, preserve opportunity, hide wounds, or maintain social order.

The central question of PersonaMirror369 is not: Who is fake?

The better question is:

> What pressure made this mask necessary, and what safety would allow truth to return?

## 2. Core Thesis

```txt
Mask ≠ Lie
Mask = Adaptive Interface
```

A mask becomes harmful when it becomes unconscious, chronic, fear-based, rigid, or fused with identity.

```txt
Protective Mask = conscious + temporary + useful + removable
Prison Mask     = unconscious + chronic + fear-based + identity-fused
```

## 3. Key Terms

### Mask

A social, emotional, cognitive, or spiritual interface used to manage how the self appears in a given context.

### Pretending Force

The pressure pushing a person away from private truth and toward performance, conformity, suppression, or false certainty.

### Truth Safety

The degree to which a person can express truth without disproportionate shame, punishment, abandonment, or survival threat.

### Coherence Delta

The distance between public performance and private truth.

### Repair Capacity

The availability of safe pathways for apology, renegotiation, boundary-setting, clarification, or reconnection.

## 5. Pretending Force Engine

The Pretending Force formula estimates social pressure.

```txt
PF = (SR + BR + PR + FR + AR + ER) - (TS + SC + PC + RC)
```

Where:

```txt
PF = Pretending Force
SR = Shame Risk
BR = Belonging Risk
PR = Punishment Risk
FR = Financial / survival Risk
AR = Approval Reward
ER = Ego Reward
TS = Truth Safety
SC = Self-Compassion
PC = Private Coherence
RC = Repair Capacity
```

Interpretation:

- Shame, belonging risk, punishment risk, survival risk, approval reward, and ego reward increase pretending pressure.
- Truth safety, self-compassion, private coherence, and repair capacity reduce pretending pressure.

## 6. Mask Load Model

```txt
ML(m) = I × F × R × D × T
```

Where:

```txt
ML = Mask Load
I  = Intensity
F  = Frequency
R  = Rigidity
D  = Distance from inner truth
T  = Time worn
```

A high Mask Load does not mean a person is bad. It means the mask may be carrying a large amount of survival, fear, expectation, identity, or social pressure.

## 7. Coherence Delta

```txt
CD = |Private Truth - Public Performance|
```

A larger Coherence Delta may indicate internal conflict, exhaustion, social fear, or role strain. The goal is not forced disclosure. The goal is to identify where truth requires safety, timing, consent, and repair.

## 8. Group Pretending Field

Groups can maintain false norms when many people privately disagree but publicly conform. PersonaMirror369 models this as a Group Pretending Field.

```txt
GPF = Σ(PFᵢ × Visibilityᵢ × Imitationᵢ × Punishmentᵢ) / TrustDensity
```

Where high visibility, imitation pressure, and punishment raise the field, while trust density lowers it.

## 11. Ethical Constraints

PersonaMirror369 must never be used for:

- forced confession
- surveillance
- deception detection
- social scoring
- employee ranking
- coercive persuasion
- romantic partner interrogation
- exposing private truths without consent

## 12. Limitations

This framework is not a clinical model, diagnostic instrument, or validated psychometric scale. The formulas are design scaffolds and reflection aids. Empirical testing would be required before making stronger claims.
